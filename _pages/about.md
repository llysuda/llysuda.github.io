I am currently an Assistant Professor at the [LIMA Center](https://www.slai.edu.cn/zh-hans/node/379) of [Shenzhen Loop Area Institute](https://www.slai.edu.cn/zh-hans). From 2017 to 2026, I worked at Huawei Hong Kong Research Center. I received my Ph.D. from Dublin City University in 2017, supervised by [Prof. Qun Liu](https://liuquncn.github.io/index_zh.html) and [Prof. Andy Way](https://www.adaptcentre.ie/experts/andy-way/), under the fund of EU Marie Curie Programme. Before that, I received my bachelor's and master's degrees from Soochow University in 2010 and 2013, respectively, supervised by [Prof. Guodong Zhou](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=KELQj9cAAAAJ&hl=en).

My research experience covers machine translation and large language models. One of our papers received an ACL 2024 Outstanding Paper Award. I have co-authored a book titled "Neural Machine Translation: Foundations, Principles, Practices and Frontiers". My team has also won championships in international challenges such as WMT and AutoSimTrans.
